Get to know Kite

Technology should bring people closer.

Kite is a messaging and social app from Kite Connect, a registered business in Lagos, Nigeria.

Who we are

Kite Connect is a registered Nigerian business based in Lagos. We build Kite, an app where people can message, share and stay in touch in one place.

We are building step by step, testing the product and listening to the people who use it. Our aim is to make digital communication feel simpler and more dependable, with real-world devices and networks in mind.

What guides us

Three ideas behind Kite.

Messaging that feels natural

Conversations and everyday communication that are straightforward and approachable.

Connectivity in mind

A thoughtful experience for people using mobile data and facing inconsistent network conditions.

A space to share

Messaging and social experiences together in one digital home.
